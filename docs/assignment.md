# Assignment of rights in a contribution to Leicht·Facile

This form goes with the section "Rights in your contribution" of
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). The maintainers ask for it signed before they
merge a substantial contribution (a new module, rule, language or letter, or more than
about 100 changed lines), and always when an organisation holds the rights in the
contribution, whatever its size. Fill it in, sign it, and send a scan or a PDF to
<hello@leichtfacile.ch>. The signed form stays with the maintainers and is not
published.

## The agreement

1. **Assignment.** The contributor assigns to the assignee the copyright and all
   related rights in the contribution, for every country and for the whole term of
   protection. Where the law that applies to the contributor does not allow this, the
   contributor grants the assignee instead an exclusive, worldwide, perpetual,
   irrevocable and transferable licence, with the right to sublicense, for every known
   kind of use of the contribution, among them its reproduction, modification,
   distribution in any form, making it available online, and its licensing to others
   under any terms, open-source or not.
2. **The contributor's own use.** The assignee grants the contributor a non-exclusive,
   worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use the contribution for
   any purpose, with the right to license it to others under any terms.
3. **Moral rights.** As far as the law allows, the contributor will not assert moral
   rights in the contribution against the assignee, the project or its users. The
   project keeps the author's name in its history.
4. **Patents.** The contributor grants the assignee and every recipient of the project
   a licence under the patent claims the contributor can license that the
   contribution, alone or combined with the project, would infringe, on the terms of
   section 3 of the Apache License 2.0.
5. **What the contributor promises.** The contribution is the contributor's own work,
   or the contribution says which parts come from elsewhere and under which licence;
   the contributor is entitled to make this assignment and these grants; and where a
   person signs for an organisation, that person is authorised to do so.
6. **No other terms.** The contribution is submitted under this agreement and the
   Apache License 2.0 and under no other terms. The project distributes it under the
   Apache License 2.0 and may distribute it under other terms as well.
7. **Law.** Swiss law applies to this agreement, without its conflict-of-law rules;
   the courts at the assignee's domicile have jurisdiction.
